Herbal study needs a better middle ground

Most herbal content is either too vague, too clinical, too academic, or too confident. WholeRootLab keeps traditional context, modern research, constituents, and safety in separate lanes so students can study with less noise.

What is inside

Three starter monographs

Birch / Betula spp.

A training herb for plant-part specificity: leaf, bark, sap, pollen, and purified compounds are not the same thing.

Meadowsweet / Filipendula ulmaria

A clean example of why "natural aspirin" is too simple and why salicylate caution matters.

Stinging Nettle / Urtica dioica

A bridge between food, herb, nutrition, and pharmacology, with root and leaf evidence kept separate.

Why Nordic herbs

Local identity, clearer evidence boundaries

Nordic and Icelandic plant knowledge is scattered across folklore, field guides, regulatory documents, and academic papers. WholeRootLab brings those layers into one student-friendly format.

  • Traditional use
  • Modern phytochemistry
  • Safety and contraindication notes
  • Research references
