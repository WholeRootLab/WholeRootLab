# WholeRootLab Validation Outreach Pack

## Goal

Validate whether herbal medicine students want an evidence-first Nordic materia medica before building a full subscription product.

Success signal for v0.1:

- 10-20 people receive the sample.
- At least 5 reply with useful feedback.
- At least 3 say they would want more entries.
- At least 1 asks how to buy, subscribe, or get the next version.

## Who To Contact First

Start with people who are close enough to understand the value but not so advanced that they expect a finished clinical reference:

- Herbal medicine students.
- People in herbal study groups.
- Plant medicine learners who care about evidence and safety.
- Nordic/Icelandic nature or herbalism communities where sharing an educational sample is allowed.
- Practitioners-in-training, not clinical authorities.

## Follow-Up After Sending Sample

Thank you for taking a look.

If you have 5 minutes, these three questions would help me most:

1. Was the format useful for studying?
2. What felt missing, confusing, or unnecessary?
3. If this expanded into a larger Nordic materia medica, which herbs would you want next?

Short, honest answers are perfect.

## What To Listen For

Strong positive signals:

- "I wish I had this for my studies."
- "Can you make one for [herb]?"
- "This is clearer than my course notes."
- "I would pay for a bigger version."
- "Do you have a Notion version?"

Weak signals:

- General encouragement with no specific use case.
- People praise the idea but do not read the sample.
- Feedback focuses only on design, not usefulness.

Product direction signals:

- If people ask for more herbs: build the 10-herb pack.
- If people ask for printable summaries: create study sheets.
- If people ask for search/filtering: invest in Notion/database structure.
- If practitioners ask about claims or compliance: keep that as a separate B2B product line later.
