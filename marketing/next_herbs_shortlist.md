# WholeRootLab v0.2 Herb Shortlist

Use validation replies to choose the next seven herbs. Do not add all of these at once until feedback comes in.

## Strong Candidates

- Yarrow / Achillea millefolium
- Angelica / Angelica archangelica
- Juniper / Juniperus communis
- Dandelion / Taraxacum officinale
- Plantain / Plantago major or Plantago lanceolata
- Lady's mantle / Alchemilla vulgaris agg.
- Seaweed entry / Laminaria or Ascophyllum context, if handled as nutrition/traditional coastal materia rather than a standard herb

## Selection Criteria

Choose herbs that have:

- Nordic or Icelandic relevance.
- Enough scientific or regulatory sources to cite responsibly.
- Clear safety-learning value.
- Student usefulness.
- Low risk of being marketed with exaggerated claims.
