## Who It Is For

WholeRootLab is for herbal medicine students, self-directed plant learners, and evidence-curious practitioners-in-training who want:

- Nordic and Iceland-relevant herbs presented in a clean study format.
- Traditional context kept separate from modern evidence.
- Plain-English explanations of constituents and research findings.
- Safety notes that do not get buried.
- References that can be checked.

## What Is Inside The Free Sample

The first sample includes three monographs:

- **Birch / Betula spp.** - a useful training herb for plant-part specificity: leaf, bark, sap, pollen, and purified compounds are not the same thing.
- **Meadowsweet / Filipendula ulmaria** - a classic example of why "natural aspirin" is too simple and why salicylate caution matters.
- **Stinging Nettle / Urtica dioica** - a bridge between food, herb, nutrition, and pharmacology, with root and leaf evidence kept separate.

Each entry includes:

- Plant identity and Nordic/Icelandic relevance.
- Traditional context.
- Key constituents in plain English.
- Evidence snapshot with simple evidence ratings.
- Safety notes.
- Student study notes.
- References and source anchors.

## Positioning Section

## Herbal study needs a better middle ground

Most herbal content is either too vague, too clinical, too academic, or too confident.

WholeRootLab sits in the middle: clear enough for students, careful enough to respect the evidence, and practical enough to become a long-term study system.

This is not medical advice. It is not a protocol guide. It is a structured way to learn what is known, what is traditional, what is still uncertain, and what deserves caution.

## Why Nordic Herbs

Nordic and Icelandic plant knowledge has a strong identity, but many resources are scattered across folklore, field guides, regulatory documents, and academic papers.

WholeRootLab brings those layers into one student-friendly format:

- Local plant identity.
- Traditional use.
- Modern phytochemistry.
- Safety and contraindication notes.
- Research references.
